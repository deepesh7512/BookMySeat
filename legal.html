Platform and transport service: Book My Seat provides booking and trip-management tools. Bus operators provide the transport service and are responsible for operating the journey.

Terms of Use

By using Book My Seat, you agree to use the booking, ticket, PNR and operations features for their intended purpose. Provide accurate account, passenger and contact details, keep your PNR and login credentials private, and follow the bus operator’s boarding instructions.

A booking is subject to the ticket status and trip details shown in the app or website. Bus operators are responsible for their buses, schedules, fares, seat availability, boarding and journey operations. Operators and crew must use passenger and booking details only to operate, support and complete the relevant journey.

For a booking or trip issue, contact the Help Desk with your PNR where available.

Privacy Policy

Information used

Depending on how you use the service, Book My Seat handles account details such as name, mobile number and email; trip searches and selected routes; passenger and seat details; PNR and booking status; and payment method, payment status or transaction reference.

Why it is used and who receives it

This information is used to sign in, search trips, create and display bookings, support passengers, and operate scheduled journeys. Relevant booking and passenger details may be shared with the bus operator and assigned crew for the booked journey. Data is stored in Supabase. When a live payment gateway is used, that provider processes payment details and may return a transaction reference and payment status; the provider’s own privacy terms also apply.

Payment channel notice

The website checkout currently states that payment is not collected on the website and shares the selected payment method with the bus operator. The Android customer app’s UPI test flow is a demo and does not collect real UPI money. Use a live gateway only when the checkout explicitly offers that live payment flow.

Retention and requests

The stated platform data-retention period is up to three months. To request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to raise a privacy concern, use the grievance or Help Desk contacts below.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Customers may request cancellation until 6 hours before the scheduled departure.
  • A 50% refund of the ticket fare is eligible only when cancellation is made at least 48 hours before the journey date.
  • If cancelled less than 48 hours before the journey but at least 6 hours before departure, no refund is due.
  • Within 6 hours of departure, customer cancellation is unavailable. Completed journeys cannot be cancelled.
  • Refunds apply only to amounts actually paid. Unpaid or pending bookings have no collected amount to refund.

If a trip is cancelled or changed by the operator, contact the Help Desk for assistance.
